The study compares modern 3D reconstruction and measurement techniques for a Byzantine bath monument. TLS, LiDAR-SLAM, photogrammetry, and mobile-device solutions were evaluated against reference measurements, with reported errors ranging from 0.01 to 3.2 cm.
