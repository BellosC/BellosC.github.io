The paper describes a 2024 summer school in Pogoni, Epirus, focused on digitizing historic stone bridges. Students and faculty from engineering and architecture collaborated across GIS, GNSS, SLAM, and cultural-heritage documentation, producing georeferenced point clouds, drawings, orthophotos, theses, and a dedicated spatial information system.
