The contribution presents the LIFE EL-BIOS digital-twin framework for forest biodiversity analysis. It combines terrestrial and airborne LiDAR, RGB and multispectral sensing, and software such as 3DFIN and 3DFOREST to extract biodiversity-related forest parameters.
