The work presents the 3D geometric documentation of the Georgios Schwarz mansion in Ampelakia, Larissa, using Leica BLK360 and RTC360 laser scanners. Deliverables included a point cloud, a 3D model, architectural drawings, and a 1:200-scale physical model.
