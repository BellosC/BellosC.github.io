The dissertation develops a LiDAR-based simultaneous localization and mapping system for three-dimensional documentation of cultural-heritage spaces. It covers SLAM foundations, system architecture, sensor integration, colourized point-cloud generation, a graphical user interface, and accuracy evaluation.
