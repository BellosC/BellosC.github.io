The study compares SLAM and TLS datasets for extracting tree height, diameter at breast height, crown metrics, and other biodiversity-relevant attributes. TLS delivered higher resolution and approximately 75% tree-detection accuracy, while SLAM achieved roughly 60–65% with greater mobility and acquisition efficiency.
