Working at CERN involves surveying in environments where precision, reliability and careful planning are essential.

My work includes geodetic metrology and alignment activities supporting accelerator and experimental infrastructure. Depending on the task, measurements may involve laser trackers, total stations, close-range photogrammetry, 3D laser scanning and geodetic network adjustment.

## Activities

- High-precision dimensional measurements
- Alignment and geometric verification
- Laser tracker measurements
- Total station surveying
- Close-range photogrammetry
- 3D laser scanning
- Point-cloud processing
- Least-squares adjustment
- As-built documentation

## Tools and technologies

`Laser trackers` · `Total stations` · `Photogrammetry` · `3D scanning` · `Python` · `Geodetic adjustment`
