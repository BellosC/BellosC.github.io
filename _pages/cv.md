# Konstantinos Bellos

**Survey Engineer, PhD**

Survey Engineer with expertise in **geodetic metrology**, **industrial surveying**, **LiDAR**, **laser scanning**, **photogrammetry**, **SLAM**, and **point cloud processing**. My work focuses on high-precision measurements for scientific and engineering applications, combining advanced surveying technologies with data analysis and software development.

My research interests include **Industrial metrology**, **Laser Scanning**, **SLAM**, **Photogrammetry**, **Cultural Heritage documentation**, and **3D printing**.
